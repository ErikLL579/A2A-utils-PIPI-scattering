# Optimized pion meson field form (momentum space)
# <w|gamma_X|v>(pos) split into <w|(pos) and gamma_X |v>(pos)
# '.' denotes matrix multiplication (A2A index contraction)
# Tr[...] denotes trace over A2A indices
#
# Optimization order (compute in this order):
#   1. prod_Pi products  — matrix-matrix (Pi chain) products (cheap, position-free)
#   2. prod_vec products — vector-matrix products (expensive, position-local)
#      Products carry their position: prod_vecN(x_1) lives at x_1
#      Products at different positions are NEVER combined
#   3. Term assembly     — combine products into final traces
#
# Momentum conventions:
#   src_1 -> (p, t_src)
#   src_2 -> (-p, t_src + Delta)
#   snk_1 -> (k, t_snk)
#   snk_2 -> (-k, t_snk + Delta)
#   x_1, x_2 -> remain in position space (summed over)
#
# =========================================================
# Phase 1: Matrix-matrix products (Pi field chains)
# =========================================================
#
# Level 1:
prod_Pi1 = Pi(-k, t_snk + Delta) . Pi(-p, t_src + Delta)    # appears 2 times
prod_Pi2 = Pi(-k, t_snk + Delta) . Pi(p, t_src)    # appears 2 times
prod_Pi3 = Pi(k, t_snk) . Pi(-p, t_src + Delta)    # appears 2 times
prod_Pi4 = Pi(k, t_snk) . Pi(p, t_src)    # appears 2 times

# =========================================================
# Phase 3: Term assembly
# =========================================================
term_ADT1_0 = coef_ADT1 * Tr[prod_Pi4] * Tr[prod_Pi1]
term_ADT1_1 = coef_ADT1 * Tr[prod_Pi3] * Tr[prod_Pi2]
term_ADT2_0 = coef_ADT2 * Tr[prod_Pi4 . prod_Pi1]
term_ADT2_1 = coef_ADT2 * Tr[prod_Pi3 . prod_Pi2]
